# Atlas Field Study instructions

Atlas is a fictional research program used to demonstrate Wizengamot. The team must decide whether a proposed field deployment is ready for a limited pilot.

- Separate observations, hypotheses, inferences, and recommendations.
- Seek disconfirming evidence before recommending deployment.
- Treat the project brief as an internal record rather than external proof.
- Preserve disagreements between source agents.
- Require measurable acceptance criteria and explicit owners.
